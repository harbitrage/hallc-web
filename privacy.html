Governance & Compliance

Privacy & Data Sovereignty.

Effective Date: August 2026. At Harrison Arbitrage LLC, data protection and intellectual stewardship are foundational to our mission of Information Sovereignty.

"Zero-trust stewardship across our publishing catalog, digital platforms, and enterprise client communications."

1. Information Sovereignty & Data Protection

We treat visitor and client telemetry with rigorous intellectual stewardship. We do not harvest, monetize, or broker personal data. All interactions across harrisonarbitrage.com are governed by zero-trust privacy principles.

2. Digital Publishing & Third-Party Distribution

Our published volumes are distributed globally via Amazon and authorized retail channels. Purchases, Kindle downloads, and paperbacks are processed securely under Amazon's privacy and retail distribution standards.

3. Subsidiary Platforms (Harrison AI Agents)

Interactions with subsidiary platforms such as Harrison AI Agents (`harrisonaiagents.com`) are governed by their respective operational security and agentic data protocols.
